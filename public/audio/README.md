# Original World of Work audio

These are original, locally composed synthetic chiptune motifs and sound effects created for this project. No commercial-game recording, sample, character theme, or third-party music was used. The authoring process used mathematical oscillators, simple additive synthesis, deterministic noise, note envelopes, and original MIDI-note sequences.

All files are mono, 16-bit PCM WAV at 16 kHz. Seven independent 7.68-second loops cover village, interior/archive, mystery, puzzle, ordinary battle, boss battle, and victory contexts. Fourteen short effects cover footsteps, interaction, menu, confirmation, collection, pages, puzzle success, unlocking, encounter reveal, attack, hit, guard, victory, and defeat.

The central audio manager fetches/decodes only requested files, starts after a user gesture, limits effect overlap, fades music transitions, and suspends playback for pause/inactive tabs. Music/SFX/master levels and mute are separate controls. File-level and manager behavior are tested; listening quality and real-device autoplay behavior still require browser/device verification.
